Emergency Hydro Jetting in Knightdale, NC

.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of a drain or sewer line, which clears what a cable only punches through. If a line has clogged more than once, or you have grease and roots in the same pipe, jetting is often the fix that holds.

Knightdale Pro Plumbing provides hydro jetting for homes and businesses in Knightdale, NC, with a plumber on call 24/7. For a first-time clog, drain cleaning with a cable is usually the right first step.

When jetting is urgent

A main line that is fully stopped, a grease-clogged restaurant drain or a floor drain overflowing are all reasons to call right away. While you wait:

  1. Stop running water in the house or kitchen.
  2. Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into the line. It will still be there when the plumber arrives.
  3. Keep people and pets away from any overflowing drain.
  4. Locate your sewer cleanout if you know where it is, and clear access to it.
  5. Call (919) 944-7281. We answer 24/7.

Cable or jetting: how we choose

Both tools have a place. We pick based on what is in the pipe.

How the process works

We start with a camera or a cable test to see what the line looks like. If jetting is safe, we feed a hose with a nozzle into the cleanout. The nozzle pushes water backward at high pressure to scour the walls and also pulls itself forward through the line.

Jetting is not right for every pipe. A fragile, cracked or badly offset line can be damaged, so a camera inspection comes before the jet whenever there is doubt. If the camera finds damage, you will hear about it before anything else is done.

Why jetting suits Knightdale's mix of older and newer homes

Older homes near downtown Knightdale, built around the railroad corridor, may have older drain materials and long runs with minimal cleanouts. Neighborhoods such as Parkside and Planter's Walk south of US 64 grew after the late-1980s Mingo Creek sewer outfall opened, though build years vary by section, so the age of your own lines is a better guide.

Raleigh Water, which serves Knightdale, names grease and wipes as common causes of sewer backups. Jetting is the tool that clears grease lining and keeps it from rebuilding quickly, while a change in habits keeps it from coming back.
