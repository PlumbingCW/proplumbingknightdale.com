Signs of a failing sewer line

Call quickly if you see any of these, especially more than one at a time:

  1. Several drains back up together, or a toilet bubbles when the washer runs.
  2. Sewage smell indoors or in the yard.
  3. A sunken or unusually green strip of lawn along the line.
  4. Slow drains that return soon after being cleared.
  5. Stop using water and call (919) 944-7281. Keep children and pets away from any sewage.

Repair methods we use

Open-trench repair is the traditional approach and often the most direct for a short, shallow break. Trenchless methods, such as lining or pipe bursting, can reduce yard damage on longer runs when the pipe condition suits them.

Which method fits depends on pipe material, depth, offsets and what sits above the line. We explain the choice and the trade-offs so you can compare it with a plain replacement.

Your pipe, the city's pipe

In Knightdale, sewer service is operated by the City of Raleigh. Raleigh Water's guidance is that the property owner maintains the sewer line from the house to the city main, and the city maintains the main. That means most repairs land on the homeowner.

Raleigh also advises asking your plumber to clear a blockage out to the city main. If our camera shows the problem is in the street, we tell you so you can report it, and you avoid paying for work that is not yours.

Why sewer lines age out in Knightdale

Older homes near downtown Knightdale, many tied to the railroad era, may have clay or cast iron laterals that crack with age and soil movement. Clay-rich Piedmont soil swells and shrinks, and roots find any small crack in search of moisture.

Subdivisions south of US 64 such as Parkside and Planter's Walk are newer, and their lines are often PVC. These problems are more often sags or poor joints than corrosion, which is a different repair.

Raleigh Water names grease and flushed wipes as two of the biggest causes of sewer backups. Good habits protect any line, old or new.

Emergency timing.

Nights and holidays are priced differently.

Insurance note: many homeowners policies exclude wear-and-tear on underground pipes, while some offer a sewer backup endorsement. Ask your insurer about both before work starts, and document any interior damage.

What to do before we arrive

Stop running water into the house. Every flush, shower and load of laundry sends more wastewater toward a blocked or broken line. Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into a sewer line, because they can burn skin and damage pipe and they rarely fix a root or collapse problem.

If sewage is on the floor, keep children and pets away and avoid contact. Open a window if there is a smell. Call us, and report anything in the street or at the city main to Raleigh Water.

Permits and licensed work

Sewer repairs are regulated plumbing work. Verify any contractor with the State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors at public.nclicensing.org. Ask the Town of Knightdale Development Services whether your project needs a permit, and know that plumbing inspections go through Wake County Permits and Inspections. We tell you up front what applies.

Slope, cleanouts and pipe materials on a repaired sewer line follow the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code, the state's edition of the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and an inspection can apply to the new work.
