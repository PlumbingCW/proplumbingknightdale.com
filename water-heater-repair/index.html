No hot water or a leaking tank: what to do first

If you smell gas near the heater, do not touch switches. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. For a water leak or a total loss of hot water, do this while you wait:

  1. Shut off the cold supply valve above the heater if there is any leak.
  2. Turn off the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas unit to off or pilot.
  3. Check the reset button and breaker only once. Repeated resets can hide a failing element.
  4. Do not drain a hot tank into a bare floor drain. The water can scald.
  5. Call (919) 944-7281 and we will talk through what you see, then send a plumber.

Gas, electric, tankless and hybrid units

Each type fails in its own way. Electric tanks usually lose an element or a thermostat. Gas tanks lose a thermocouple, an igniter or a gas control valve. Tankless units usually fail from scale, flow sensors or ignition problems, and error codes tell us where to start.

Heat pump and hybrid units add a compressor and fan to the electric tank, and they need clear air space to work. Hybrid repair is mostly diagnostics, so we ask for the model and any error code when you call.

Water temperature and safety checks we do on every visit

Repair visits are also a safety check. We look at:

  • The temperature and pressure relief valve and its discharge pipe.
  • Gas connections, venting and combustion air on gas units.
  • Expansion tank condition on closed systems.
  • Shut-off valve operation, so you can stop a leak next time.
  • Whether the anode rod has worn enough to affect the tank.

A gas heater with poor venting can put carbon monoxide into the house. The Consumer Product Safety Commission advises CO alarms on every level of the home and outside sleeping areas, and interconnected alarms are best.

Hybrid, electric and gas water heater repair

Repair advice depends on the fuel. A gas unit and an electric unit of the same age call for different checks, and a hybrid heat pump unit needs different tools again. We bring a multimeter for electric units, a combustion analyzer when gas venting is in question and the right diagnostic tools for heat pump models.

If a repair would cost most of what a new unit costs, we say so. Water heater installation is a better use of money than repairing a tank that is past its service life.

Knightdale water and your heater

Knightdale gets its water through the City of Raleigh. Raleigh Water's November 2025 finished water report shows total hardness of 23.9 mg/L as calcium carbonate at the plant tap, which is soft water. Scale is less aggressive here than in hard-water regions, but sediment and anode wear still happen over the years.

If you live in an older railroad-era house near downtown, check the vent and the relief valve discharge line as well. Older installs sometimes have added or altered pipework that no longer meets current practice. For leaks outside the heater itself, see leak detection.

If a repair touches the relief valve, its discharge line or the venting, it should meet the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code, the state's edition of the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and we check those items on every visit.
