Main line backup: what to do now

A backed-up main line sends wastewater to the lowest drain in the house. Act quickly:

  1. Stop running every water source, including washers, dishwashers and showers.
  2. Do not flush toilets. Each flush adds water with nowhere to go.
  3. Protect the lowest area. Lift valuables and rugs away from the tub or floor drain.
  4. Find the sewer cleanout, usually a capped pipe outside near the foundation, and clear the area around it.
  5. Call (919) 944-7281. We answer 24/7.

How we clear the line

We open the cleanout and run a cable machine with the cutting head that suits the blockage. After the line is open, we flush it with water and check that it flows freely to the city connection.

If the line clogs again within months, we recommend a camera inspection. Roots, a sag or a cracked joint will keep catching debris no matter how often a cable clears it.

Why main lines clog in Knightdale

The usual causes are simple, and many of them are preventable:

  • Grease poured down kitchen drains and cooled into a coating.
  • Wipes sold as flushable, which do not break down like toilet paper. Raleigh Water lists them among the leading causes of backups.
  • Tree roots entering at joints in older clay or cast iron pipe.
  • A sag in the line where solids settle.
  • Items that should have gone in the trash, such as paper towels and hygiene products.

Raleigh Water serves Knightdale, and its guidance is to wipe grease from pans with a paper towel and keep wipes out of the toilet.
