Why emergency plumbing in Knightdale looks the way it does

Knightdale grew up around the railroad, and the older homes near the downtown rail line were built for railroad workers. Houses of that age often have original or early-replacement drain lines, aging water heaters and shut-off valves nobody has turned in decades. Those are the calls where a camera and an honest look at the pipe save the most money.

South of US 64, subdivisions such as Parkside, Planters Walk and Mingo Creek were built after the Mingo Creek sewer outfall opened in the late 1980s. Build years vary from section to section, so the age of your own water heater, supply lines and fixtures is the better guide than the subdivision name.

Local note

Who owns which pipe in Knightdale

Knightdale's water and sewer are provided by Raleigh Water. The city maintains the main in the street and the meter. As a general rule, the property owner maintains the line from the meter to the house and the sewer line from the house to the city main.

Raleigh Water also advises asking your plumber to clear a sewer blockage out to the city main, and names grease and wipes as leading causes of backups. If the problem is in the street, we tell you so you can report it. Our sewer line cleaning page explains how that works.

Source: Raleigh Water service and sewer guidance.
