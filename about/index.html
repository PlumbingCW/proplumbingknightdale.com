Who we are

Knightdale Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service for Knightdale, NC. We handle emergency and planned plumbing for Knightdale and the nearby towns of Wendell, Zebulon and Rolesville in eastern Wake County.

Our phone line is answered around the clock. When you call, you reach a person who asks what is happening, gives shut-off guidance and sends the closest available plumber.

How we work

We find the cause before we recommend a fix. A clogged drain gets cleared and then we look at why it clogged. A failing heater gets a repair if a repair will last, and a replacement plan if it will not.

We quote a clear price before work starts, we explain the choices in plain language and we do not push work that you do not need. If a repair is not worth it, we say so.

Licensing and how to check it

Plumbing contractors in North Carolina are licensed by the State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors. You can check any contractor, including us, on the board's public license search at public.nclicensing.org.

Permit and inspection rules depend on the job. The Town of Knightdale Development Services can tell you whether your project needs a permit, and plumbing inspections are handled through Wake County Permits and Inspections. We explain what applies before work starts.

Where we work

We serve Knightdale, ZIP 27545, plus Wendell, Zebulon and Rolesville. Water and sewer service in all four towns comes from Raleigh Water, so the rules about who owns which pipe are the same everywhere.

What we handle

Emergency calls make up the work people remember: sewage backups, burst pipes, leaking water heaters and drains that have stopped completely. We respond to those day and night, and we start by telling you how to limit the damage while a plumber is on the way.

The rest is planned work. That includes water heater replacements and installs, tankless service, leak detection, sewer inspections, repiping, fixtures and remodel plumbing. Our plumbing services page lists all of it, and our commercial plumbing page covers businesses.

What to expect on a visit

The plumber introduces themselves, looks at the problem and finds the cause before talking about a fix. You get a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead.

When the work is done, the plumber tests it, cleans up and explains what failed and what to watch for. If we spot a bigger issue, such as an aging line or a heater near the end of its life, we tell you what it is and how urgent it is, and we leave the decision with you.

What we will not do

We will not promise an arrival time we cannot keep, quote a price we cannot explain or recommend a repipe when a repair will hold. We also do not invent reviews, awards or numbers to look bigger than we are.

If a job is outside what we do, or the cause is not plumbing, we say so and point you in the right direction.

Knowing the local housing

Knightdale has two housing eras side by side. The older homes near the downtown rail line were built for railroad workers, and subdivisions south of US 64 such as Parkside, Planter's Walk and Mingo Creek grew after the Mingo Creek sewer outfall opened in the late 1980s.

That matters because pipe material, drain layout and heater age follow the era. A plumber who knows which houses tend to have which problems finds the cause faster and spends less of your time on guesses.
