What we know about Planters Walk

Planters Walk is a recognized Knightdale subdivision, listed with ZIP code 27545. Real-estate listings we reviewed place homes on streets such as Beddingfield Drive, Trail Stream Way and Tarford Place. Those listings describe a community pool, playgrounds and access to the Mingo Creek Trail, which links to Raleigh's Neuse River Trail.

Neighborhood directories list sections including Landings at Planters Walk, Oaks at Planters Walk, Applegate, Willow Ridge, Mathews Glen, Woodberry and Ashebrook. Those are separate builds, so two houses a few streets apart may differ in age.

Why the age of your own house matters more

A general history of Knightdale notes that Parkside, Planter's Walk and Mingo Creek were built after the Mingo Creek sewer outfall opened in the late 1980s. The listings we reviewed show homes built between 1988 and 2006.

We do not claim that homes of any age have particular pipe materials or problems. What we do say is that build years vary by section, so the data label on your own water heater and the dates on your fixtures are a better guide than the subdivision name.

Read the water heater label

The label lists the model and serial number, and the serial number usually encodes the manufacture date. If your tank is more than ten years old, plan ahead. Our water heater replacement page explains how to plan the swap.

Watch for slow drains

A single slow drain is usually local to that fixture. When several drains slow down at once, the problem may be farther down the line. See drain cleaning for what we check first.

Hidden leaks show up on the bill

A water bill that rises with no change in habits can point to a leak. Our leak detection page explains meter checks and how leaks are found without tearing into walls.

Local note

Raleigh Water and the pipes you own

Raleigh Water provides water and sewer service in Knightdale. As a general rule, the city maintains the main and the meter, and the property owner maintains the line from the meter to the house and the sewer lateral from the house to the city main. Ask Raleigh Water to confirm where your responsibility starts. Plumbing work here follows the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code.

Raleigh Water's report for November 2025 listed total hardness of 23.9 mg/L, which is considered soft water. That means scale is usually less of a worry than in hard-water areas, though sediment can still collect in a tank.

Sources: Raleigh Water, Wikipedia on Knightdale, real-estate listings for Planters Walk.
