Where Mingo Creek is

Mingo Creek Park is at 100 Parkside Commons Drive. The Town of Knightdale describes it as an 8.2-acre site with a dog park, half-court basketball, a playground and paved trails, and lists 4.75 miles of trails and greenway in and around the scenic wetlands and hardwood forests. The greenway links to Raleigh's Neuse River Greenway Trail and to Knightdale Station Park.

A local real-estate guide places the Mingo Creek neighborhood between the Knightdale bypass and I-540 and describes it as townhomes and single-family homes. The Town's project list also shows Mingo Creek Phases 7 and 12, a 98-townhome project, under construction with inspections ongoing.

Plumbing in townhomes and attached homes

We do not claim that Mingo Creek homes have a specific plumbing problem. The point here is about how attached homes work, which is general plumbing information.

In a townhome, a leak can reach a neighbor through a shared wall or ceiling. Before you hire anyone, check your homeowners association documents and insurance policy to see who is responsible for which pipes and for repairs to shared structure.

Find your shut-offs

Know where the main shut-off is for your unit and where the shut-offs sit under sinks and behind toilets. If you cannot find them, a plumber can show you on a visit.

Fixtures and supply lines

Braided supply lines under sinks and behind toilets can fail without warning. See faucet repair and toilet repair for what we check and when replacement makes sense.

Leaks you cannot see

Water stains on a ceiling or a damp spot on a shared wall deserve attention. Our leak detection page explains how leaks are located with minimal damage.

Local note

Raleigh Water and the pipes you own

Raleigh Water provides water and sewer service in Knightdale. As a general rule, the city maintains the main and the meter, and the property owner maintains the line from the meter to the house and the sewer lateral from the house to the city main. Ask Raleigh Water to confirm where your responsibility starts. Plumbing work here follows the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code.

In a townhome community, the association may own or insure some lines. Your association documents are the place to confirm that, not this page.

Sources: Town of Knightdale Mingo Creek Park and project list pages, Raleigh Water.
