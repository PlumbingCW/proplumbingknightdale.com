A town that more than doubled in ten years

Rolesville was incorporated in 1837, which makes it the second-oldest town in Wake County, but most of what you see today is new. The 2020 census counted 9,475 people, up from 3,786 in 2010, a 150 percent increase, and Wikipedia's 2025 estimate is 12,839.

That growth means a large share of Rolesville homes were built after 2000. Newer houses usually have PEX or CPVC supply lines and PVC drains, which avoid the corrosion problems of older metal pipe. They bring their own issues. Shut-offs were never exercised, original water heaters are now ten to fifteen years old and fixtures were chosen to a budget.

Local note

Raleigh Water and Rolesville homes

Raleigh Water lists Rolesville among the communities it serves. Its November 2025 finished water report shows total hardness of 23.9 mg/L as calcium carbonate at the plant tap, which is soft. Soft water slows scale on heaters and fixtures, so newer Rolesville homes tend to be less scale-limited than hard-water areas.

That does not make a heater immortal. Anode rods wear and sediment settles, and a ten-year-old tank deserves a look. The water heater installation page explains how to plan a replacement before the old one fails.

Newer Rolesville homes were built to the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code, the state's edition of the International Plumbing Code (IPC) of their day, and we repair and replace to the current one.

Source: Raleigh Water finished water report, November 2025.

Why drains in a new subdivision clog

New does not mean clog-proof. Construction debris can stay in a line, and grease in the kitchen line coats even brand new PVC over time. Raleigh Water names grease and wipes as leading causes of sewer backups.

Rolesville's parks and growth along Main Street and the school area mean more households sharing the same sewer system, which makes everyone's habits matter. If a kitchen sink keeps slowing, hydro jetting clears the coating that a cable leaves behind.

What decides the price of a Rolesville call

What to do before we arrive

Close the main water valve if water is flowing, and stop using drains if one is backing up. Rolesville homes served by Raleigh Water have a meter at the street, and the owner is generally responsible for the line from the meter to the house.

Photograph any damage before cleanup, and move rugs and electronics out of the wet area. If you smell gas near the water heater, leave and call from outside.
