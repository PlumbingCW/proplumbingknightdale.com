Where Downtown Knightdale is

A general Knightdale history identifies US 64 Business, known locally as Knightdale Boulevard, as the main commercial district. It places Knightdale Station Park on the east side of town, across First Avenue from the older downtown businesses.

The Town also has a planned mixed-use project called Downtown North at Knightdale Station. A 2022 sketch plan describes about 91 acres with a grocery, a fitness facility, a hotel, shops and professional offices alongside townhomes, condos and single-family lots. We treat it as a plan, since we could not confirm what has been built.

Commercial plumbing questions

We do not claim to know the condition of any building's plumbing. These are general points for commercial properties.

Restaurants and food service need to manage grease. Retail and office spaces often have restrooms that see heavy use, and a shared water heater or shared drain can affect several tenants at once.

Grease and drains

Grease builds up in kitchen lines and can cause backups. Raleigh Water names grease as a leading cause of sewer backups. See hydro jetting and drain cleaning for how blockages are cleared.

Water heaters and restrooms

A failed water heater or a leaking restroom fixture can close a space. Our commercial plumbing page covers what we handle for businesses.

Tenants and landlords

Check your lease to see who is responsible for plumbing repairs. A landlord may need to authorize work, so confirm before you call a plumber.

Local note

Utilities and permits for businesses

Raleigh Water provides water and sewer service in Knightdale. As a general rule, the city maintains the main and the meter, and the property owner maintains the line from the meter to the house and the sewer lateral from the house to the city main. Ask Raleigh Water to confirm where your responsibility starts. Plumbing work here follows the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code.

Commercial plumbing is licensed work in North Carolina, and tenant improvements often need a permit. Ask your plumber whether a permit applies and confirm with Wake County Permits and Inspections and the Town of Knightdale Development Services.

Sources: Wikipedia on Knightdale, Town of Knightdale sketch plan, Raleigh Water.
