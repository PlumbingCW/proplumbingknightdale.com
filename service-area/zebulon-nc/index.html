Pipe burst or sewage backing up in Zebulon: first steps

Whatever the cause, these steps limit the damage while the plumber is on the way:

  1. Close the main water valve, or the valve at the meter if the house valve will not turn.
  2. Stop using every drain if sewage is backing up. Each flush adds more water to the line.
  3. Turn off breakers for rooms with standing water and stay out of them.
  4. Lift valuables and rugs away from the lowest point in the house.
  5. Call (919) 944-7281. Our Zebulon dispatch is open 24/7.

A railroad town, and what its age means for pipes

Zebulon was chartered on February 17, 1907, after investors in the Raleigh and Pamlico Sound Railroad, later part of the Norfolk and Southern line, platted the town. The rail line was moved about a mile and a half south of its original path through the older settlement of Wakefield, and Wakefield was later annexed by Zebulon in 1997.

Zebulon's own chamber history notes that electricity arrived in 1916 and that a water system was approved by 1919. A town that early built its water and sewer a century ago, and houses from those decades often still have fixtures, shut-offs and drain lines from several rounds of repairs.

The practical result is a simple checklist for older Zebulon homes: find the main shut-off, check the water heater's age and ask for a camera look at the sewer line before a small slow drain becomes a backup.

Local note

Raleigh Water serves Zebulon

Raleigh Water lists Zebulon among the communities it serves, so the water main in the street and the meter belong to the City of Raleigh. The line from the meter to your house and the sewer lateral from the house to the main are generally the owner's responsibility.

If the problem is in the street, report it to Raleigh Water. If it is on your side, call us. We will tell you which side it is on before any digging, and the sewer inspection page explains how a camera settles the question.

Repairs in Zebulon's older houses are brought up to the North Carolina State Building Code: Plumbing Code, the state's edition of the International Plumbing Code (IPC), so an old fixture is replaced with a current one.

Downtown Zebulon and the Wakelon School

Wakelon School, which opened in 1908 and was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1976, now serves as Zebulon's Town Hall. Buildings like it, and the shops around the downtown, are examples of older construction where pipework has often been modified over the decades.

For businesses in older buildings, the useful step is a quick plumbing walkthrough: floor drains, restroom shut-offs, the water heater and any grease-producing equipment. Our commercial plumbing page covers what we check.
